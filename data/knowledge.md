## About Digital Security Solutions
Digital Security Solutions (DSS, also known as Digital Security India) is a Delhi-based supplier and installer of security and surveillance systems, established in 2008 (18+ years of experience). Tagline: "Surety of Security". We serve homes, residential societies, shops, offices, factories, warehouses, schools, hospitals and government organisations across Delhi NCR and India. We are MSME registered (Ministry of MSME, Govt. of India), ISO 9001:2015 certified and listed on GeM (Government e-Marketplace).

## How pricing and quotations work
Prices depend on the number and type of cameras or devices, resolution, recorder and storage, cabling length, site layout and installation work. We do not quote fixed prices in chat. Customers can get an accurate quotation by booking a free site survey on the enquiry page (/enquiry), using the "Get Price" button on any product page, calling +91 99996 05550 or messaging on WhatsApp.

## Free site survey
We offer a free site survey: our technician visits, checks camera/device positions, cabling and power, and recommends the right setup, followed by a quotation. Book it at /enquiry or by phone/WhatsApp.

## Free security audit for societies and builders
Residential societies and builders can request a free security audit covering CCTV, access control, boom barriers, visitor management, intercom/video door phones, networking and PA systems. Request it on the enquiry page (/enquiry) by choosing "Free Security Audit (Society / Builder)".

## CCTV installation, repair and AMC
We supply and install HD and IP CCTV cameras with DVR/NVR recorders, hard disks, PoE switches, cabling and mobile app viewing. We also repair existing CCTV systems (including ones we did not install) and offer AMC (annual maintenance contracts) for regular check-ups and priority service. Remote viewing on mobile is set up during installation.

## Access control, turnstiles and boom barriers
Access control for single doors to multi-site facilities using PIN, RFID card, fingerprint and face recognition. Automated boom barriers for vehicle entry (with RFID readers, loop detectors and controllers), turnstiles (tripod, speed gate, full-height) to stop tailgating, and electromagnetic, solenoid or smart digital locks. Reports show who entered which area and when, with alerts for forced doors.

## Biometric attendance
Fingerprint and face recognition attendance machines (eSSL and others) for offices, factories and schools, with attendance reports and integration with access control.

## Video door phones and intercoms
Video door phones let you see and talk to visitors before opening the door, from an indoor monitor or a phone. Intercom systems for homes, societies and offices.

## Intrusion and fire alarm systems
Intrusion alarms with motion sensors, door contacts and smart hubs for 24/7 alerts, wireless alarm kits for homes and shops, and addressable fire detection panels for buildings.

## Gate and home automation
Motorised sliding and swing gates and boom barriers with remote and sensor control. Home automation for lights, appliances, climate and security, controllable from anywhere.

## PA systems and AV
Public address systems, mixers, amplifiers, wall and ceiling speakers and AV controls (Bose Professional, JBL Professional and others) for offices, venues, schools and places of worship.

## IT, networking and power backup
EPABX / IPABX telephone systems, FTTH fibre networks, Cat6 structured cabling, racks and patch panels, online UPS with battery banks so cameras keep recording during power cuts, and solar power for remote camera locations.
